1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy

Okto Latuperissa ("Developer", "we", "our", or "us") built the Remote TV AC Free application (Package ID: com.omniremote.universal) as an ad-supported All-in-One Universal Remote & Multi-Tool utility app. This service is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

Core Privacy Principle: We do NOT operate any proprietary cloud user-tracking database, do NOT require user registration or login accounts, and do NOT upload your camera feed, microphone audio, NFC card data, notes, or local home device list to our servers. All utility tools execute locally on your Android device.

2. Device Permissions & On-Device Processing

To deliver Universal Remote, NFC Reader, Sensor, Camera, and Audio Studio utilities, the app requests certain optional hardware permissions strictly when needed:

  • Infrared Transmitter (TRANSMIT_IR): Used locally to emit 38kHz–56kHz infrared remote control signals to TVs, Air Conditioners, and Projectors.
  • Local Wi-Fi & Bluetooth Discovery (NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES, BLUETOOTH_SCAN, BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE): Used strictly on your local network to discover and send remote commands to Smart TVs, Smart Bulbs, and Bluetooth HID devices. We never collect or transmit your Wi-Fi passwords or device list externally.
  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Required by older Android versions (Android 6–11) for local BLE/Wi-Fi scanning and used locally on-device to display real-time speed (km/h) in the GPS HUD Speedometer tool. Your GPS coordinates are never stored on or sent to our servers.
  • NFC Reader (NFC): Used locally when you tap a physical contactless smartcard/e-Toll card to inspect public card balance/UID metadata on screen. Scan history is stored exclusively inside your phone's local storage and can be deleted anytime with one tap.
  • Camera (CAMERA): Used strictly in real time on your device screen for the Digital Magnifier, HD Makeup Mirror, Live RGB Color Picker, and Framing Grid tools. No photos or videos are recorded, saved externally, or uploaded anywhere.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Used strictly in volatile device RAM for the Acoustic Decibel (dB) Meter, Vocal Pitch Tuner, and temporary 4-second RAM Voice Changer playback. Audio is never saved permanently or transmitted off your device.

3. Advertising, Google AdMob & Advertising ID

Remote TV AC Free displays advertisements (Banner Ads, Native Advanced Ads, and Rewarded Ads) provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to keep all 208+ features free for users. Where required by law (including EEA/UK GDPR), we use the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to gather user consent prior to serving personalized ads.

Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain non-personally-identifiable technical data, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): Used for ad delivery, frequency capping, rewarded unlock verification, analytics, and fraud/abuse prevention. You may reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in your Android Settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Device & Network Diagnostics: Approximate IP-based region, device model, OS version, screen dimensions, ad impression/click events, and crash diagnostics transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections.

4. Local Data Storage, Retention & Deletion

Any saved remotes, pinned tools, quick notes, cashflow entries, or NFC card history you create are stored strictly locally on your own device via Android SharedPreferences / offline SQLite databases.

How to Delete Your Data: Because no personal data is stored on external developer servers, you have 100% immediate control. You can delete individual saved remotes/NFC cards directly inside the app, clear Application Storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app to permanently erase all local data.

5. Children's Privacy

This application is designed for general audiences aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly target or collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

2. Penggunaan Izin Perangkat (Permissions)

  • Inframerah (TRANSMIT_IR): Memancarkan sinyal remote inframerah langsung dari sensor HP ke TV, AC, dan Proyektor.
  • Wi-Fi Lokal & Bluetooth (NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES, BLUETOOTH_SCAN, BLUETOOTH_CONNECT): Memindai dan mengontrol Smart TV / perangkat pintar di jaringan rumah lokal Anda.
  • Lokasi GPS (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION): Digunakan secara lokal di perangkat untuk menghitung kecepatan km/jam pada fitur Speedometer GPS HUD serta pemindaian perangkat lokal pada Android versi lama. Titik lokasi Anda tidak pernah dikirim ke server kami.
  • NFC (NFC): Membaca informasi publik saldo/UID kartu elektronik e-Toll saat kartu ditempelkan ke HP Anda. Riwayat kartu tersimpan lokal di HP Anda dan dapat dihapus kapan saja.
  • Kamera (CAMERA): Digunakan murni secara langsung di layar untuk fitur Kaca Pembesar Digital, Cermin Rias HD, Detektor Warna RGB, dan Grid Foto tanpa merekam atau mengunggah foto/video.
  • Mikrofon (RECORD_AUDIO): Digunakan sementara di memori RAM perangkat untuk fitur Pengukur Kebisingan Desibel (dB), Detektor Nada Vokal, dan Pengubah Suara instan tanpa disimpan permanen atau dikirim ke internet.

3. Layanan Iklan Pihak Ketiga (Google AdMob)

Aplikasi ini menayangkan iklan melalui Google AdMob agar seluruh fitur dapat dinikmati secara gratis. Anda dapat mengatur ulang atau menghapus ID Iklan kapan saja melalui menu Pengaturan Android → Google → Iklan.
